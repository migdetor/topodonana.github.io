# Cartographic information

This page brings together the geographic information layers and associated resources used in the updated map of Doñana National Park. For each layer you will find a **Factsheet** (documentation) and, where applicable, a **Download** link.

---
 
## Quick access

- **Download** (stable package): [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18109683)
- ***How to cite***: M. de Felipe (2026). Updated Map of Doñana National Park. Zenodo. [DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18109683](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18109683)
- **Versions and changes** (changelog): [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/USUARIO/REPO/releases)

---

## Scope and recommended use

- **Spatial coverage**: Doñana National Park and the restored marsh (*marisma gallega*) within Doñana Natural Park. Toponymy also includes the immediate surrounding area.
- **Reference system (CRS)**: ETRS89 / UTM zone 29N (EPSG:25829).
- **Resolution / effective scale**: integrated product ~1:49,800. Each layer may have its own effective scale (see factsheets).
- **Recommended use**: consultation, synthesis, ecological/territorial analysis.  
 *Not suitable for boundary demarcations or legal decisions.*
- **Temporal coverage**: version V1 (2026). Specific temporal components are documented per layer.

>**Note:** Due to the potentially sensitive nature of some of the information contained in these layers, certain datasets are not available for direct download. However, the author may provide them upon request once the intended use of the data has been stated.
